## Mistakes to emphasize

- Calling Incomplete a failure.
- Using the requested schedule as approved while Pending.
- Treating exactly 24 hours as less than 24.
- Penalizing a tester-requested change.
- Creating a duplicate when status looks stale.

## Closing summary

“Verify the schedule and requester, apply the exact boundary, copy the temporary post, and treat the time as tentative until the status becomes Approved.”
