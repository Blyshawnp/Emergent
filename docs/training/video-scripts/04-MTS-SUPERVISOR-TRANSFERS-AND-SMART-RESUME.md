## Objectives

- Complete the normal Supervisor Transfer flow.
- Choose Smart Resume instead of an unnecessary fresh transfer-only session.
- Handle resumed NC/NS and final-attempt outcomes correctly.

## Prerequisites

Synthetic normal-flow session; local resumable history; shared pending transfer example; final-attempt example.

## Demo data/setup

Use distinct synthetic candidates for normal, resumed, and final-attempt scenes. Include saved call results and coaching in the resume example.

## Mistakes to emphasize

- Starting fresh when Smart Resume data exists.
- Loading the wrong shared pending candidate.
- Omitting fail reasons or Other notes.
- Treating prior technical issues as current NC/NS evidence.
- Describing an incomplete follow-up as a failed certification.

## Closing summary

“Use normal flow for same-session work, Smart Resume for saved calls, and Supervisor Transfer Only only when no resumable record should be used. Confirm the candidate and attempt state before scoring.”
