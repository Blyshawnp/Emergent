## Mistakes to emphasize

- Copying a similar-looking post without previewing.
- Assuming post and screenshot copy together.
- Pasting training content into a live chat.
- Memorizing defaults without checking customized shortcuts.

## Closing summary

“Search, preview, copy, and use the current Shortcuts reference. When the workflow is uncertain, open the matching Help article before posting.”
