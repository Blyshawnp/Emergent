# MTS Mock Calls

## Metadata

- Title: MTS Mock Calls: Accurate Results, Reasons, and Coaching
- VideoKey: `mts-mock-calls`
- Category: Mock Calls
- HelpTopicKey: `calls`
- Audience: MTS trainers and evaluators
- Duration: 7-9 minutes

## Objectives

- Record Pass/Fail, coaching, and fail reasons accurately.
- Use Other and added detail correctly.
- Distinguish NC/NS, Same Day Drop, Stopped Responding, and Tech Issue.

## Prerequisites

Synthetic active session on Call #1; safe caller/show/donation options; one pass and one fail example.

## Demo data/setup

Use training-only payment simulation and synthetic caller data. Prepare a fail reason with optional detail and Other with required notes.

## Recording checklist

- [ ] No real caller/candidate/payment data.
- [ ] Coaching and fail reason examples are factual and concise.
- [ ] Session-ending confirmations can be shown without saving production data.

## Mistakes to emphasize

- Passing a call while recording fail-only facts.
- Selecting generic reasons without needed detail.
- Using Other when a listed reason fits, or leaving Other notes blank.
- Selecting NC/NS for a candidate who attended and later dropped.
- Using Stopped Responding for a system problem.

## Closing summary

“For every call, complete the setup, choose the true result, record actual coaching, and fully explain a failure. Then let MTS route the next step.”
