# MTS Quick Start

## Metadata

- Title: MTS Quick Start: From Home to History
- VideoKey: `mts-quick-start`
- Category: Quick Start
- HelpTopicKey: `getting-started`
- Audience: New MTS trainers
- Duration: 5-7 minutes
- Recording impact: partial scene replacement for Lookup and Basics.

## Objectives

- Recognize the core MTS workflow and where each stage lives.
- Know when to use Smart Resume and Supervisor Transfer Only.
- Understand that Form Filled is not submitted.

## Prerequisites

Completed setup; synthetic candidate; safe completed History record.

## Demo data/setup

Use `Jordan Training` with an approved demo headset and no real candidate history. Preload one synthetic completed record for the History scene. Do not run a real form submission.

- [ ] Candidate and tester values synthetic.
- [ ] No private account or browser data visible.

## Mistakes to emphasize

- Starting a new session when Smart Resume is available.
- Selecting a candidate without reviewing the match.
- Treating technical signals as automatic decisions.
- Calling Form Filled submitted.
- Refilling a record after a status-warning success.

## Closing summary

“Remember the route: verify in Basics, record the calls, complete or schedule the Supervisor Transfer work, verify Review and Form Fill, then confirm the saved status in History.”
