# Default Supervisor Coaching Checkboxes

Master source:

- `backend/content/app_content.json`

## Current defaults

1. `Minimize dead air`
   Helper:
   - `Maintain engagement throughout hold and transfer`
2. `Queue Not Changed`
   Helper:
   - `Did not change queue to ACD Direct Supervisor`
3. `Caller Placed On Hold`
4. `Verification`
   Children:
   - `Name`
   - `Address`
   - `Phone`
   - `Email`
   - `Card/EFT`
5. `Discord permission`
   Helper:
   - `Ask explicit permission to transfer via Discord`
6. `Did not notify caller of transfer`
   Helper:
   - `Notify caller before transferring`
7. `Screenshots/Discord Chat`
   Helper:
   - `Coached with standard instructions and screenshots`
8. `Search name for every call`
   Helper:
   - `Search the caller's name on every call to avoid duplicate member records.`
9. `Do not volunteer information`
   Helper:
   - `Do not verify details the member has not provided, such as an email address.`
10. `Other`
